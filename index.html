DMG Research Hub

Open trials, data resources and primary literature for H3 K27M altered diffuse midline glioma, kept in one place.

Compiled by Arun Kumar Anguraj Vadivel, Molecular Medicine, SPARC Innovation Hub, The Hospital for Sick Children, Toronto. Trials and newly published papers refresh automatically each day from ClinicalTrials.gov and PubMed; the curated sections are reviewed by hand. Page built 26 October 2026. This page is a research reference, not medical advice; treatment decisions belong with a patient's own care team.

Data and resources

Where DMG tissue, molecular data and reference material actually live. Access terms differ: some are open, some controlled, and some require a project application.

Tissue, genomics and clinical data

  • Children's Brain Tumor Network (CBTN)Specimens, clinical annotation and harmonized sequencing from member institutions; request access through the CBTN platform.
  • Kids First Data Resource CenterHosts the CBTN dataset and other pediatric cohorts for cloud analysis.
  • PedcBioPortalBrowser for pediatric cancer genomics, including OpenPBTA and CBTN cohorts.
  • Childhood Cancer Data CatalogIndex of childhood cancer datasets and the access route for each.

Proteomics and molecular archives

  • Proteomic Data CommonsCPTAC proteogenomic data, including the pediatric brain tumor cohort.
  • PRIDEProteomeXchange archive for raw and processed mass spectrometry data.
  • European Genome-phenome ArchiveControlled access archive for human sequence and molecular data.
  • Human Protein AtlasTissue, subcellular and single cell type protein localization references.

Models, dependencies and classifiers

  • DepMapCancer dependency maps, including the pediatric dependency panel.
  • Molecular Neuropathology classifierDNA methylation based CNS tumor classification, including the DMG K27 class.

Consortia, registries and families

  • ChadTough Defeat DMG FoundationResearch funding, the DMG ACT advocacy effort, and the My DIPG Navigator support service for families.
  • The Cure Starts NowFunds DIPG and DMG research internationally and convenes the DIPG/DMG Collaborative.
  • DIPG/DMG CollaborativePooled funding partnership of family foundations supporting DIPG and DMG research worldwide.
  • DIPG.org research overviewPlain language summaries of current DIPG research directions for families and clinicians.
  • Michael Mosier Defeat DIPG FoundationGrants to DIPG research programs, including early support for ONC201 development.

Grants and foundations

Where DMG specific research money comes from, and the Canadian brain tumour funders that also support this work. Deadlines move each year, so check each call directly.

Disease specific foundations

  • DIPG/DMG CollaborativeJoint grant calls funded by a coalition of family foundations; the largest dedicated DIPG and DMG funder.
  • The Cure Starts Now research grantsOpen calls for DIPG, DMG and homerun cancer research, with international chapters.
  • ChadTough Defeat DMG Foundation awardsNew investigator and fellowship awards in DIPG and DMG research.

Canadian and general brain tumour funding

Preprints and blogs

Work that has not been through a journal yet, and the places where people write about the field between papers. Preprints are not peer reviewed; read them as claims rather than conclusions.

Preprints and unpublished work

  • bioRxivSearch “diffuse midline glioma” for preprints of primary research. Note that bioRxiv does not accept opinion or narrative review pieces.
  • medRxivClinical and trial related preprints, including early reports of DMG studies.
  • Research SquarePreprints posted during journal review, often with reviewer comments attached.
  • ZenodoGeneral repository with DOIs for preprints, datasets, code, posters and slides that have no journal home.
  • OSF PreprintsMulti-discipline preprint search across many community servers.

Writing about the field

  • Springer Nature Research CommunitiesBehind the paper posts and commentary written by authors of recent cancer and neuroscience papers.
  • ChadTough research updatesLay summaries of newly funded DIPG and DMG projects and trial progress.

About this page

This is a personal research reference maintained alongside work on DMG proteomics and functional omics at SickKids. It is not affiliated with any consortium, registry or sponsor, and inclusion here is not an endorsement of a trial or a product. Trial status changes constantly, so treat the ClinicalTrials.gov record as current and this table as a snapshot.

Corrections, additions and links worth including are welcome: arun.angurajvadivel@sickkids.ca

Compiled 26 October 2026. Trial data from ClinicalTrials.gov; literature metadata from PubMed. Families looking for support and current treatment options should start with their treating centre and with the foundations listed above.
